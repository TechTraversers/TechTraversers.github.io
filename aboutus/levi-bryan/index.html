My faith is an important part of who I am and influences how I approach life and my work. I strive to be thoughtful, honest, and helpful while continuing to grow in knowledge and character.

Overall, I'm someone who enjoys taking on challenging projects, learning new skills, and using technology to create practical solutions that benefit others.
