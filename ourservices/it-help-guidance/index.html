IT help and guidance

Computer problems are easier to work through when someone takes the time to explain things. I help with setup, troubleshooting, and everyday technology questions.

Help at your pace

You do not need to know technical terms to ask for help. Tell me what you are trying to do and what is going wrong. I especially enjoy helping older adults get more comfortable with technology.

What I help with

  • Setting up a computer and getting started with it.
  • Troubleshooting software, settings, and everyday computer problems.
  • Understanding how to use the technology you already have.
  • Working out whether an existing tool or a custom solution would help.

Arranging help

I am based in Buffalo, Wyoming, and offer remote help worldwide. In-person help is available locally by arrangement. Email a short description of the issue, the device you use, and anything you have already tried so we can discuss the best way to help.

Back up important files before troubleshooting, and never email passwords or payment details. If something is outside my experience, I will say so.
