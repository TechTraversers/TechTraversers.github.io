IT Help — For Anyone!

We help you work through software problems, settings, and everyday technology questions. Tell us what device you use, what went wrong, and what you have already tried.

Pricing and availability

Our rate is $25 per hour for support, including calls, messages, active troubleshooting, and email. Contact us to confirm availability, the time estimate, and any in-person visit costs before work begins.

We are based in Buffalo, Wyoming, and also serve parts of Rapid City, South Dakota. Remote help and in-person visits depend on the issue and our availability.

How it works

  1. Email us with a short description of the problem.
  2. We discuss whether we can help and agree on an appointment and costs.
  3. We work through the issue with you and explain the next steps.
  4. Pay using PayPal, cash for local visits, or check. PayPal is preferred.

Before we start

Back up important files before troubleshooting. Never email passwords or payment details. Some issues may need a specialist or replacement hardware; we will discuss those limits with you.

We may decline work outside our skills or availability. Confirm the current service terms with us when booking.
