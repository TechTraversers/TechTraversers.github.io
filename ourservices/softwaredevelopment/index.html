Software Development

We build small Python and C++ applications for focused tasks, such as automating a repeated process, organizing information, or creating a simple desktop tool or game.

Pricing based on time

Custom software development is $25 per hour. The cost depends on how long the work takes. There are no fixed prices for individual applications.

Tell us what you need, and we will discuss the scope and estimated hours before starting. If the requirements change, we will discuss how that affects the time needed.

What we can help with

Tell us the task you want to accomplish and the operating system you use. If suitable software already exists, our IT help service may be a better fit than building something new.

Scope, compatibility, and updates

Our focus is on small, practical tools. We agree on the essential features and target operating system with you. Ongoing maintenance and additional features are separate work; operating system changes may also require updates.

If something does not match the agreed scope, contact us so we can review it. Discuss support and update costs before ordering.

Software already in use

Our Wood Bank Software is currently used by Clear Creek Wood Bank and is available for other interested organizations. Contact us to discuss using it.

How long does it take?

The time required depends on the features, complexity, and testing involved. We discuss estimated working hours and a delivery timeline after reviewing your request. We are not full-time developers, so working hours and calendar time are different; we will explain both when discussing the project.
